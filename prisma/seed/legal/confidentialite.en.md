## Data controller

[[ÉDITEUR]], publisher of Kiraku. Contact for any question about your data: [[CONTACT]].

## Data collected and why

- **Account**: username, e-mail address and password, stored as a hash and never in plain text (e-mail account), or Discord id, username and avatar (Discord sign-in). *Purpose*: create and secure your account. *Legal basis*: performance of the terms of use.
- **Game**: cards, opened boosters, Zeni and their ledger, auctions and bids, battles, missions, friends, gifts, review, wishlist, preferences (language, profile visibility). *Purpose*: run the game and keep the economy fair. *Legal basis*: performance of the terms of use.
- **Security**: IP address and technical connection data. *Purpose*: limit abuse (mass account creation, bots, cheating). *Legal basis*: legitimate interest in protecting the service.
- **Performance measurement**: page load and response times (Vercel Speed Insights), without cookies or identifiers. *Legal basis*: legitimate interest in keeping the site fast.
- **Push notifications** (if you turn them on): your browser's subscription address. *Legal basis*: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from your Account.

Kiraku sells no data, shows no advertising and does no profiling.

## Retention periods

- Account and game data: as long as the account exists. After a deletion request, the account is anonymized after 30 days.
- Notifications: 30 days.
- IP address used against abuse: a few hours at most in the rate-limiting service; hosting logs according to Vercel's retention.
- The Zeni ledger and cards of a deleted account are kept **without any link to a person**, for the integrity of the game economy.

## Processors

- **Vercel** (website hosting) — United States, running in the London region.
- **Supabase** (database) — region [[RÉGION_SUPABASE]].
- **Resend** (verification and password e-mails).
- **Ably** (real-time battles).
- **Discord** (sign-in, if you choose it).

Some of these providers are established in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights

At any time you can:

- **access** your data and **take it with you**: Account → My data → Download my data (JSON file);
- **correct** it: username, language and visibility in your Account;
- **delete** your account: Account → My data → Delete my account;
- **object** to processing or **ask for its restriction**, and withdraw your consent to push notifications.

For any other request, write to [[CONTACT]]. Answer within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority): https://www.cnil.fr/en/
