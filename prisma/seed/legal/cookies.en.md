# Cookie policy

A cookie is a small file stored in your browser; local storage is its browser-side equivalent. Kiraku uses as few as possible.

## What Kiraku uses

Only cookies and storage that are **necessary** for the site (keeping your session open, securing Discord sign-in, remembering a referral link) or for **your preferences** (theme, language, sound). They are exempt from consent: no banner asks for it.

Kiraku sets **no advertising, tracking or social network cookie**, and does not measure its audience with trackers.

The full list, with the role and duration of each, is right below. It is generated from the site's code, so it is always up to date.

## If this changes

If Kiraku ever adds a tracker that requires consent (non-exempt audience measurement, for example), a banner will ask for your choice before anything is stored, with "Accept all" and "Refuse all" equally visible. Your choice will be asked again every 6 months and can be changed at any time with the "Manage cookies" button at the bottom of every page.

## Deleting cookies

You can delete cookies and local storage from your browser settings. Deleting the session cookie signs you out; if you were playing as a guest without creating an account, your progress can no longer be recovered.
