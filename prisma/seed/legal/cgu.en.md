## 1. Purpose

Kiraku is a free collectible card game featuring anime characters: opening boosters, collecting, trading with friends, a Zeni auction house and battles. These terms govern its use. By playing, you accept them; by creating an account, you accept them expressly.

## 2. Age

Kiraku is meant for people aged **15 and over**. If you are under 15, you need the consent of a parent or guardian to use the game.

## 3. Account

- You can play as a guest, then create an account (e-mail or Discord) to keep your progress. A guest account with no visit for 7 days is deleted along with its progress.
- **One account per person.** Multiple accounts created to stack boosters, Zeni or cards are forbidden.
- You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for what happens on your account.
- Your username must not be insulting, discriminatory, misleading or impersonate anyone.

## 4. Zeni, cards and boosters

- Zeni, cards and boosters are game items with **no monetary value**. They cannot be refunded, converted into money or transferred outside the game.
- Selling, buying or trading accounts, Zeni or cards for real money or any item outside the game is forbidden.
- Booster drop rates are shown in the game. The server alone decides draws, prices and battle results.
- The publisher may change the game balance (rates, prices, rewards) at any time.
- **Casino.** The casino coin flip pits two players who each stake a card of the same tier; the winner keeps both. **Neither Zeni nor real money** can be staked, and nothing is sold: only virtual cards with no monetary value change hands. The server draws the result and anyone can verify it: its fingerprint is published when the table opens, and the seed is revealed after the flip. A staked card stays locked while its table is open and goes back to its owner if nobody takes it in time.
- **Kyara import.** A player can request, once and within the displayed delay, the import of their Kyara collection from an account linked to the same Discord account as on Kyara. Each character becomes the matching base card, as a single copy; a card rarer on Kiraku than on Kyara, already owned or missing from the catalog is converted into Zeni according to the displayed scale. A Kyara profile or a Discord account can only be used for one import. The team may decline a request it cannot verify. Kiraku is not affiliated with Kyara.
- **Referrals.** You can refer a friend who creates their account with your link or your code; a friend can only be referred once. Gifts for both of you are paid automatically at each step they reach (account created, boosters opened, days played), according to the amounts displayed at the time of payment, and milestones reward the number of settled friends. For 14 days after they arrive, your friend can neither sell nor give away their cards, and card trades between the two of you are blocked for 30 days. The team may put a referral under review (shared device, suspicious shared activity): nothing is paid until it is approved.
- **Ennichi.** The Ennichi is a time-limited festival, on the dates shown in the game. Each friend who arrives during the festival and settles lights a lantern; at each lantern tier reached, every player with a registered and verified account who opened at least one booster during the festival receives the tier's prize, within a limit of a few accounts per IP address. The banzuke ranks referrers by the friends who arrived and settled during the festival; on a tie, the one who reached their count first comes first. At closing, the omikuji draws prizes among the tickets (one per friend settled during the festival), rarest first, each ticket winning at most one prize; the draw is verifiable (seed hash published when the festival starts, seed revealed after the draw). A referral still under review at closing counts neither in the banzuke nor in the omikuji. Prizes are game items with no monetary value.
